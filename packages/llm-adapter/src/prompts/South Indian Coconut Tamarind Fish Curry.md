South Indian Coconut Tamarind Fish Curry    

Ingredients :

500 g fish, cut into medium pieces
1 drumstick, cut into 5 cm pieces
1/2 raw mango, cut into chunks
1/2 cup grated coconut
2 tbsp coconut oil
5 fresh red chillies
2 tsp chilli powder
1 tsp coriander powder
1 tsp turmeric powder
1 tsp mustard seeds
1/2 tsp fenugreek powder
1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
1 small onion, thinly sliced
4 cloves garlic, minced
1 inch ginger, grated
1 lemon-sized ball tamarind
10 curry leaves
Salt to taste
2 tbsp fresh coriander, chopped

Method :

Soak the tamarind in warm water for 10 minutes and squeeze out the juice.

Heat coconut oil in a clay pot over medium heat.
Add mustard seeds and let them crackle.
Add fenugreek seeds and curry leaves and fry briefly.

Add the sliced onion, garlic and ginger and cook until softened.
Add the fresh red chillies, chilli powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder and fenugreek powder.
Stir briefly without burning the spices.

Add the raw mango and drumstick.
Pour in the tamarind extract and add salt to taste.
Bring to a gentle boil and simmer until the vegetables are partly tender.

Add the fish pieces carefully and simmer until the fish is cooked through and the gravy thickens.
Do not stir aggressively after adding the fish.

Add the grated coconut near the end and simmer briefly.
Finish with fresh coriander and serve hot with rice.
